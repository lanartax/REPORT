Logika & Rumus Perhitungan Dashboard Sosmed

1. TIMEGONE & Status Emotikon

Timegone adalah persentase hari berjalan terhadap total hari bulan tersebut:

Timegone = (Hari Berjalan / Total Hari Bulan) × 100%
  • 🖤 On Track: Capaian ≥ Timegone (atau toleransi ≥ 38%).
  • 💔 Warning: Capaian antara 35% – 37%.
  • ☠️ Critical: Capaian < 35%.

2. Target Gross Profit (GP) 5%

Target GP = 5% × Target Omzet Bulanan

Contoh: Target Omzet Rp 1.081.261.572 × 5% = Rp 54.063.079.

3. Target Rasio Cross Selling 1 : 3

% CROSSELLING = MIN( 100%, (Jlh Cross >50K / Jlh Gadget) × 3 )

Jika mencapai minimal 1 cross sell per 3 gadget (33,33%), maka KPI Cross-selling mendapat nilai 100%.
